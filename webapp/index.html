How good is it?

Walk-forward backtest: every season from 2017-18 on is predicted by a model trained only on earlier seasons. Log loss scores the probabilities (lower is better). Shootouts count as a coin flip.

Accuracy by confidence tier

Each pick's tier comes from how confident the model is in the favorite. Stronger tiers are right much more often, but they cover fewer games. "All seasons" is the full backtest since 2017-18.
